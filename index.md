Quick Cite Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-24

A tool pops up references - MLA 9, APA 7, or Chicago - when you feed it website addresses. Pages get cited without digging through rules. Formats adjust behind the scenes while you move on.

Data we process
Quick Cite can access:
The page address and name at the moment of citation.
Web page details help shape how references appear. Think of titles, writers, when it went live, where it's hosted - those bits matter. Information tucked into code, say JSON-LD, plays a quiet role too. Each piece slots in place behind the scenes. Formatting leans on these cues without calling attention. The structure follows what’s embedded, nothing more. Clarity comes from matching parts others might overlook.
A single click on a link might be enough for Quick Cite to stay idle - unless the address sits there, unclicked, just pasted as text. When a URL appears without being opened, the tool can reach out to that web location. It pulls only what it needs: details about the source. The moment it sees a DOI or webpage address left untouched, fetching begins. Information flows back not because asked but because present. Quiet access replaces manual entry each time.

Data we store
Your info stays right inside the browser, kept safe by Quick Cite:
Keeps your citation collections right where you left them - tagged, labeled, close at hand. Projects reappear just as you set them up. Recent picks show up without extra steps. Everything stays put until you decide otherwise.

Data We Do Not Collect Or Share:
With Quick Cite, everything stays on your device. Nothing travels to outside systems. Your information never leaves your control. All processing happens right where you are. There is no connection made beyond your screen.
Folks who use Quick Cite can relax - personal info stays put. No handing it off to outsiders, ever. What you share remains yours alone, locked away from external reach.
With Quick Cite, there's no hidden monitoring. Tracking tools for ads stay turned off completely. Analytics never collect your activity here.

Permissions

Extract metadata from current page or chosen URL to create citations.
Your preferences stay on the device. Saved choices remain right where you left them.
